What I learned

  1. Own your documents. Do not assume the centre will attach everything. Emailing extras was not enough for me; handing them in physically got things moving.
  2. Track it yourself. Check the status portal regularly, and don't rely on customer care.
  3. Apply early. I applied about two months before travel, which gave me breathing room.
  4. Don't guess from finances. Our financial profiles differed and I was the one asked for extra documents, yet both of us were approved.
  5. Go straight to VFS Delhi for extras. Walk-ins are accepted. Take the Enquiry token to skip the long queue and submit documents in one visit. In my estimate, handing them in at Gurgaon would have added 3 to 4 days. Other centres may need an appointment (opens in new tab).

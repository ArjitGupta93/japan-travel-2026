We left Delhi on 28 September and landed at Haneda on the 29th, after a layover in Hong Kong. We flew home from Osaka on 8 October, via Bangkok.

We went in early autumn: warm days, a lot of rain, and maples that were still mostly green. Peak colour usually arrives later, around mid-November to early December in Kyoto and Tokyo.
